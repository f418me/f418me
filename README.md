# Micropayments, AI & Agentic Payments

AI agents are becoming active participants in digital markets. They need payment rails that are instant, programmable, global, and cheap enough for machine-to-machine use.

f418.me builds and explores micropayments, agentic payments, and AI-native business models, from aSentrX AI Trading to Lightning-enabled tools for agents, APIs, and digital content.

## What we explore

- **Agentic Payments** — How AI agents can pay for tools, data, and services within defined budgets.
- **Pay-per-use APIs** — Micropayments for individual requests, inference, and machine-to-machine services.
- **Paid Digital Content** — Lightning-enabled access to articles, files, and other digital resources.
- **AI-driven Markets** — Experiments in market analysis and autonomous trading.
